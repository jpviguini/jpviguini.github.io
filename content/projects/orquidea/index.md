## About

**Orquidea** is a ReactJS web application that integrates with the [ORCID API](https://orcid.org/) to provide a user-friendly platform for exploring academic researcher profiles and metrics.  

While ORCID provides a powerful database of academic information, its interface can be limited for everyday exploration. Orquidea focuses on improving usability and design, offering a clean and intuitive platform to navigate researcher profiles, visualize metrics, and discover collaboration opportunities.

The goal is to provide a simpler, more engaging, and user-friendly interface for ORCID, making academic data exploration accessible and visually appealing.

This project is fully open-source, and the source code is available on [GitHub](https://github.com/EngSoft2025/orquidea-project).

---

## Features

- 🔍 **Researcher Search**: Search for researchers by ORCID ID or name.  
- 📊 **Metrics Dashboard**: Interactive visualizations of citations, publications, and collaborations.  
- 👥 **Researcher Comparison**: Compare two researchers side by side to analyze impact and production.
- 🌍 **Top Articles by Field**: Explore the most relevant articles within a specific research area.
